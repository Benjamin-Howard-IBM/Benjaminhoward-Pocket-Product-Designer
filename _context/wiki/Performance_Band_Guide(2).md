# Performance Band Guide

## Designer 7

### Scope of Influence
Focuses on team and department objectives.

### High Level Description
Works on special projects or leads small teams or manages tactical technical and operational activities or departments.

### Business Outcomes
Understands and articulates the main business outcomes for their product, service or initiative.

### Skills
**Design Specialty**
- Begins to develop Experienced level skills in a core design specialty with understanding of other design disciplines.
- Develops Foundation level skill for IBM Experience Standards, IBM Design Language/Brand, and Carbon Design System.

### Cross Functional
- Develops Experienced level skills in Enterprise Design Thinking.
- Applies user/client insights.
- Begins to lead advocacy of design thinking within the team and across disciplines.
- Participates in Agile delivery and collaborates across teams.

### Domain / Industry
- Intersects core discipline skills and domain expertise.
- Deepens understanding of users and clients.
- Articulates differentiation.
- Develops Foundation level technical understanding.

### Behaviors
#### Growth Minded
Begins to build a mentorship network and participates in design community activities.

#### Trusted
Contributes to a psychologically safe environment.

#### Team Focused
Begins mentoring teammates and shares expertise.

#### Courageous
Anticipates challenges and mitigates risks.

#### Resourceful
Demonstrates ability to exceed expectations with available resources.

#### Outcome Focused
Continuously diagnoses situations and revises the path forward.

### Persona Examples
#### Top Performer
A top-performing Band 7 designer consistently delivers high-impact work, influences product outcomes, mentors others, builds strong cross-functional partnerships, and demonstrates leadership beyond their immediate responsibilities.

#### Core Performer
A core-performing Band 7 designer reliably delivers quality design work, collaborates effectively, communicates design rationale clearly, and continues developing influence and leadership skills.

#### Low Performer
A low-performing Band 7 designer struggles to connect work to business outcomes, collaborate effectively, adapt to change, and demonstrate the leadership expected at the band.

## Designer 8

### Scope of Influence
Guides functional objectives within department.

### High Level Description
Provides leadership and guides functional objectives or technologies while understanding client needs and delivering business value.

### Business Outcomes
Understands and demonstrates how individual efforts delivered business outcomes.

### Skills
**Design Specialty**
- Consistently demonstrates Experienced level skills in a core specialty.
- Develops wing disciplines as part of a T-shaped skill set.
- Demonstrates Experienced proficiency in IBM Experience Standards, IBM Design Language, and Carbon Design System.

### Cross Functional
- Leads advocacy of Enterprise Design Thinking practices.
- May lead execution activities and Agile workflows.
- Collaborates across teams.

### Domain / Industry
- Combines domain expertise with design skills.
- Deepens understanding of users and clients.
- Articulates differentiation.
- Builds technical understanding for collaboration with SMEs.

### Behaviors
#### Growth Minded
Builds a robust mentorship network and begins leading design community activities.

#### Trusted
Provides direct feedback and supports a safe environment.

#### Team Focused
Mentors teammates and shares expertise broadly.

#### Courageous
Encourages risk-taking and communicates a clear perspective.

#### Resourceful
Uses data to enable faster decision making.

#### Outcome Focused
Makes hard decisions to fail fast.

### Persona Examples
#### Top Performer
A top-performing Band 8 designer drives measurable business outcomes, influences adoption of new practices, mentors others, and is recognized as an expert in their craft.

#### Core Performer
A core-performing Band 8 designer consistently delivers high-quality work, leads initiatives, builds strong stakeholder relationships, and demonstrates leadership through collaboration and mentorship.

#### Low Performer
A low-performing Band 8 designer struggles to operate at the expected leadership level, focuses primarily on individual contribution, and has difficulty influencing broader outcomes.
